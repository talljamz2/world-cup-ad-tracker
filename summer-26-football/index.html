How we score

The Composite Score is a single 0–100 number summarizing how a campaign is performing across the dimensions marketers actually care about. Each factor is normalized against the leader in the dataset, then combined using the weights below.

We split the leaderboard into two tiers — Official Partners (brands with a contractual sponsorship at any tournament rights tier) and Other (brands running tournament-coded creative without an official deal). The classification follows the brand's parent company, so Powerade reads as Official via Coca-Cola, and Mastercard reads as Other because the payment-category exclusivity belongs to Visa.

What we measure. We count the campaign's full paid distribution — the brand's own video posts on YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok, plus the paid talent reposts that are part of the same campaign (cast members posting the same creative on their own channels around the release window). We deliberately exclude photo posts and image carousels: those don't expose public view counts, so they can't be measured consistently. Organic talent reshares, fan content, and unrelated celebrity enthusiasm are also out — only commissioned campaign distribution counts.

Reach
45%
Cross-platform views across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok — each log-scaled and normalized within its own platform. Distribution breadth is itself rewarded: not posting on a platform contributes nothing for that third of the score.
Resonance
25%
Engagement rate (likes + comments per view) computed per platform, normalized within each platform's max, averaged across all three. The quality signal that paid amplification can't fake.
Velocity
30%
Cross-platform views per day — trailing-7-day rate summed across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. Captures campaign momentum, not just lifetime accumulation. Log-scaled so paid-amp trajectory can't dominate.
