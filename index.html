About

The Ad Podium is an independent editorial publication covering brand creative at the world's biggest sporting events. We track every major campaign — from Official Partners with multi-year deals to challenger brands running tournament-coded work without one — and rank them on cross-platform reach, engagement rate, and momentum.

No commercial relationship with any tracked brand. Editorial review on every ad before it lands on a leaderboard. Methodology documented openly inside each tracker.
